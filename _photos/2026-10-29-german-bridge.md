Located in the Gölbaşı district of Adıyaman, Türkiye, this historical railway bridge was built in 1929 by German engineers as part of the Fevzipaşa-Kurtalan railway line.

Registered as a protected cultural heritage site, it stands as a remarkable example of early 20th-century engineering in the region.
